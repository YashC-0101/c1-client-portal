The one worth telling you about

Your Aircall account isn't only yours

When we got in to set things up, we found four numbers on that account — C1 Clearance, and three lines belonging to completely unrelated businesses: a clinical line and two site-services firms.

That matters because of how Aircall works. It sends call and message alerts for the whole account, not per number. Connected as it was, the first call to that clinical line would have opened a lead in C1's inbox — their caller's phone number, their voicemail, their recording, sitting in your system.

We found it before switching anything on, and fixed it the same day. The Lead Centre now ignores anything that didn't come in on C1's own number. We tested it: a call on the clinical line is refused, the identical call on C1's line opens the lead properly.

Worth knowing either way — if that account is shared with other businesses on purpose, it's worth a conversation with whoever administers it.

What we fixed today

A tester went through every screen on a phone and a desktop. Twenty-one findings, all done.

  • Buttons did the wrong thing. "Send chaser" didn't send a chaser — it quietly moved the job to Booked. "Price it up" moved it to Site Visit Required. Every button now does what its label says, or says what it actually does.
  • Every time was an hour out. An enquiry that came in at 17:57 was shown as 16:57. Now correct, and it handles the clocks changing in October by itself.
  • The page was wider than the screen on a normal laptop. Anything between a small laptop and a big monitor scrolled sideways, cutting off the Sign out button and the end of the pipeline. Fits properly now.
  • Notifications were chopped off on phones. The panel ran off the edge of the screen and every line lost its first few words.
  • The "next step" notes were stale. A finished job still said "Confirm Thu 8:30", and one quote said to chase it on a date before the enquiry even arrived. They're worked out from the job now, so they can't go stale.
  • The Numbers page was telling you something untrue. It stated "Forwarding is already live" while the setup step for forwarding hadn't started, and listed all four channels under a heading saying "CONNECTED" while Settings correctly said none were. Both now read the real state. This was the one we least wanted left in.
  • Text you couldn't comfortably read. Timestamps, "Unassigned", the green stage labels and the red "Overdue" were all too faint to meet the accessibility standard. Every word on every screen now passes.
  • The phone version was the poorer product. It hid the "next action" column — the one that tells you what to do — and you couldn't start a quote from a phone at all. Buttons were too small to hit reliably. All fixed, and a conversation now has noticeably more room for the messages.
  • Small things that add up. Refreshing no longer throws you back to the Dashboard, the Back button works, rows that look clickable now open the lead, the dashboard counts stopped calling finished jobs "live", developer notes came off the screen, and the app can go on a phone's home screen.

Two more things to be aware of

  • Your new Aircall number is 0333 091 3602. That's not the 0330 133 9144 on the van. That's per the plan — the van's number gets forwarded to it now and moved across properly in about a month — but worth confirming that's what Sam intended, rather than the 0330 being moved directly.
  • There's still some test data in the live system. One lead shows as finished three times over, and a stray "hi" someone typed while testing is sitting as a lead's summary line. Harmless, but untidy. We've built the tool to clear it — it just needs running by whoever has access to the database, and it shows you exactly what it'll remove before it removes anything.
