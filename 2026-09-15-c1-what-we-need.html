WhatsApp without Facebook

Sam was clear he doesn't want the Facebook route, so we worked out where that leaves us. You can have WhatsApp in the Lead Centre without ever logging into Facebook. Three ways to go:

What we'd do ~£45/mo

We buy the number through a provider that's licensed to run WhatsApp for you. There is an account on Meta's side — there always is, for any WhatsApp that isn't just someone's personal phone — but we set it up and we hold it. No login for you, no documents, no queue. One catch: until a business is verified there's a limit on how many new people you can message a day. We think around 250. At forty enquiries a week you wouldn't get near it, and we'll confirm the number before we buy anything.

Get verified later only if you ever want it

Verification shows your business name instead of a bare number and lifts that daily limit. It needs C1's documents going to Meta, which is the bit Sam doesn't want. Skip it for now. Revisit if the volume grows.

No Meta at all £0 extra

Drop WhatsApp and run on calls and texts through Aircall. Nothing touches Meta in any form. Given Dan's two enquiries in eight months, that's more sensible than it sounds — say the word and we'll price it that way.

One to say no to. If anyone offers WhatsApp with no Meta involvement at all, it's a tool that logs in as a normal user. It breaks WhatsApp's rules and gets numbers banned with no appeal. The proper providers aren't avoiding Meta — they're just dealing with it so you don't have to.
