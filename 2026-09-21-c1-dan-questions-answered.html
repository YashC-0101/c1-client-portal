On not missing leads

This was the bit underneath all four questions, so it's worth answering on its own.

The worst way a system like this fails is quietly — a setting changes, enquiries stop arriving, and every screen carries on looking completely normal. You'd find out a fortnight later.

So there's a monitor built in that watches for exactly that. If a connected channel goes silent during working hours, it goes red and we get told — and it's clever enough to ignore evenings and weekends, so it isn't crying wolf at 9pm on a Saturday.

The honest caveat: that only works for channels that are actually connected. Today that's none of them. Which is the real answer to "am I missing leads" — you're not missing them in the Lead Centre, because nothing is going into it yet.
