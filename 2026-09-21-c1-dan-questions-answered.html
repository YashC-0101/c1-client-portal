On not missing any leads

This was the bit underneath all four questions, so it's worth answering on its own.

The worst way a system like this fails is quietly. A setting changes, enquiries stop arriving, and every screen carries on looking completely normal — you'd find out a fortnight later.

So there's a monitor built in that watches for exactly that. If a connected channel goes quiet during working hours it goes red and we get told, and it's sensible enough to ignore evenings and weekends so it isn't crying wolf at 9pm on a Saturday.

The honest caveat: that only works for channels that are actually connected, and today that's none of them. Which is really the answer to "am I missing leads" — you're not missing them in the Lead Centre, because nothing's going into it yet.
