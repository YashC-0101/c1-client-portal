C1 Lead Centre — feasibility & build plan TECHNICAL

Verdict: buildable — with three changes to the original plan. (1) Don't port the WhatsApp number — WhatsApp is tied to the number, not the SIM, and the number is printed inside the QR code on the van, so a failed re-verification is a re-wrap, not a settings change; Meta's Coexistence route brings it in on its existing SIM with the chat history intact. (2) A divert carries calls, not WhatsApp — the two are separate jobs. (3) Six weeks, not 21 days — Aircall's own docs for UK ports, but the divert makes recording live in week one. Plus the three-asset ledger, what Coexistence costs you in features, running costs including the 1 October 2026 Meta pricing change, an 8-row risk ledger and the compliance points on call recording.
