What the tester found, and what we did

Two rounds, twenty-six findings. Twenty fixed and live, listed here so you can check us.

The five biggest
  • Buttons did the opposite of their label. "Send chaser" sent nothing and quietly marked the job booked. Every button now does what it says.
  • Every time was an hour out. An enquiry at 17:57 showed as 16:57. Correct now, and it handles the October clock change by itself.
  • The reply box vanished on a laptop. At common laptop and iPad widths the box you type into had no room left — just a send arrow. This came from our own fix the week before, which stopped the page scrolling sideways but pushed the squeeze inside the cards. Properly fixed and checked at every width from 901 to 1440.
  • The next-step notes had gone stale. A finished job still said "Confirm Thu 8:30"; a quote said to chase it on a date before the enquiry arrived. They are worked out from the job now, so they cannot drift.
  • The Numbers page was telling you something untrue. It said forwarding was already live when the step had not started. It reads the real state now — and so does the Integrations panel, which was calling Aircall "not connected" while it was.
Reading and using it
  • Text you could not comfortably read. Timestamps, "Unassigned", the green stage labels and the red "Overdue" all failed the accessibility standard. Every word on every screen passes now, checked by measurement rather than eye.
  • Buttons too small to hit on a phone. Several were tall enough but too narrow. All clear the 44-pixel minimum in both directions.
  • The phone version was the poorer product. It hid the "next action" column — the one telling you what to do — and you could not start a quote from a phone at all. Both back.
  • Columns hidden with no way to reach them. On All Leads, Stage and Next Action simply disappeared on a narrower screen. They scroll now.
  • Three date styles on one screen. Everything absolute reads like "1 Sep 2026". The two deadline counters also counted different kinds of day without saying so; the one that skips weekends now says "working days".
Things that quietly misled
  • Times stopped updating on an open page. A dashboard left alone said "19h" for half an hour after it should have said "20h". It refreshes itself now, and again whenever you come back to the tab.
  • A failed page load looked like a business with no customers. If the page could not reach the server it showed zeros everywhere, which reads as a quiet day rather than a broken page. It says so plainly now, with a Try again.
  • The dashboard cut off stages and told you to scroll when there was nothing to scroll to. It measures itself; the hint appears only when something really is out of view.
  • A counter said 11 and opened a page showing 6. The All leads card now opens the list it is counting.
  • Test data was indistinguishable from real. Every invented lead carries a TEST label wherever it appears. The one that matters most from here: when your first real enquiry lands, you will know it is real without recognising a name.

Also fixed: day dividers now carry dates, so a message from four weeks ago cannot read as this week's; dates no longer break across two lines; rows that looked clickable open the lead; refresh and the Back button work and there is a proper 404; developer notes came off the screen; the keyboard focus ring on the dashboard; a button in the conversation header that did nothing; the app can go on a phone's home screen; and the sign-in page no longer tells Sam to ask Sam for a password reset.

One we caught before it bit

Clearing out the old demo data left the Lead Centre empty, which was the point — so your first real enquiry arrives into a clean inbox. But the app rebuilds its starter data whenever it finds an empty database, and the next routine update would have poured nineteen invented customers straight back in, alongside anything real that had arrived.

Found and fixed the same day. Worth telling you because it is the kind of thing that would have been very confusing a week later.

Still outstanding — and why

Six of the twenty-six. None stops the Lead Centre working. Two of them we have deliberately not decided on our own, and they are in the questions below.

  • A conversation is still cramped on a small phone. Needs your call Why not done: we got the message area from about 250 pixels to just over 300, but getting properly more space means taking something else off the screen — either the menu bar along the bottom, or the WhatsApp / SMS / Email buttons above the reply box. Both are things you use. We are not guessing which one you would rather lose. See the question below.
  • The unread badge is per browser, not per person. Needs your call Why not done: read your notifications on the laptop and your phone still shows them unread. Fixing it means the app remembering what each person has read, which is a change to how it stores data — about half a day, and it touches the database. Worth doing, but not worth doing quietly in the middle of go-live week. See the question below.
  • "1 lost" is text, where every other figure is a link. Why not done: small, and it was the lowest-value thing on a long list. About an hour. We will do it unless you say otherwise.
  • Settings forgets which tab you were on. Why not done: refresh while looking at Integrations and it returns to General. Same fix we applied to the main tabs last week, just not carried into the sub-tabs yet. An hour or so.
  • Settings → General does not say who can change things. Why not done: it shows your account, the team and the 30-minute target, all read-only, without saying who to ask to change them. The honest fix depends on your answer about the shared hello@ login — if everyone shares one account, "who can change this" has a different answer. Waiting on that rather than writing something we would have to undo.
  • The notifications panel does not hold keyboard focus. Why not done: opening it with a keyboard and pressing Tab walks out of the panel instead of around it. Escape does close it. Matters for anyone using the app by keyboard; a couple of hours.

Everything else the tester raised across both rounds is fixed and live — the list is above.
