Photos now stay put

This was the big one. You quote off photos, and until this afternoon a picture sent over WhatsApp would have arrived and been thrown away.

  • Photos show up in the conversation, as thumbnails you can click to open full size.
  • Someone sending three photos and no words is now a normal enquiry. It used to be ignored for having no text.
  • Call recordings and voicemails are kept too. The thread had a "Play recording" line on it from day one with nothing behind it — there is now.

One decision for you, and it is worth making before real photos start arriving. What we keep is a link to the phone company's copy of the picture, not the picture itself. If they ever delete or expire theirs, the thumbnail stops working and you are left with the filename. Holding your own copy means paying for somewhere to put them — a few pounds a month, most likely. Tell us if you want that and we will price it properly.

And it will tell us if it breaks

  • The worst way for this to fail is quietly — a password changes at Aircall's end, enquiries stop arriving, and the inbox just looks like a slow day. Nobody notices until a customer rings to ask why they were ignored.
  • The app now watches for that and raises the alarm when a connected channel goes silent for too long.
  • It counts working hours only, so a quiet evening or a Sunday doesn't set anything off.
